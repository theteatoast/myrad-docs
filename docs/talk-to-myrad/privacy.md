# Privacy & Safety

Talk to Myrad is built so that privacy is a property of the system, not a promise on a page. These are the guarantees you can rely on.

---

## The crowd never exposes a person

Crowd answers are **aggregates only**: counts, averages, distributions, popular items. They never surface a single person's record. Ask about the crowd, and you get patterns, never individuals.

## Your data needs your sign-in

Personal answers are returned **only for the verified, signed-in account**. No name, email, or wallet is ever exposed to anyone else. Your data is yours alone.

## No passwords handed over

You verify what is yours **once, in the MYRAD app**. Talk to Myrad never takes your passwords or full account access; it works from verified data, not your credentials.

## Payments are trustless

Credit purchases are **verified on-chain**. The app checks the real USDC transfer on Base rather than trusting the client, so you are only ever credited for a payment that actually happened. See [Credits & Billing](./credits).

## Your wallet stays your wallet

Your wallet is used **only** to verify your MYRAD holdings and to buy credits. Signing in costs no gas and never moves your funds.

---

:::info The short version
The crowd shows patterns, never people. Your data answers only to you. Your credentials never leave your side. Payments are proven, not trusted.
:::
