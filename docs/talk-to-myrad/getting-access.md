# Getting Access

Access to Talk to Myrad has **two distinct parts**. Keeping them separate is the key thing to understand:

1. **Holder access**: required for *all* chat.
2. **Personal sign-in**: required only for the *"Your data"* mode.

---

## 1. Holder access (required for all chat)

To use Talk to Myrad, connect a crypto wallet that **holds MYRAD**. You then **sign a message** to prove you own the wallet.

:::info Signature only
Proving ownership is a signature only: no gas, and no funds move. Your wallet is used to verify your holdings, nothing else.
:::

New holders receive **free starter credits** to begin chatting right away. You need to hold MYRAD to have access.

---

## 2. Personal sign-in (required only for "Your data")

To talk to your *own* data, also **sign in to your MYRAD account**. This tells Talk to Myrad whose data to read, and it only ever reads yours.

The **crowd** mode never needs this sign-in, and never sees any individual.

| You want to... | Wallet (holder access) | MYRAD sign-in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask about **the crowd** | ✅ Required | Not needed |
| Ask about **your data** | ✅ Required | ✅ Required |

---

## Sessions

Sessions expire after a period of inactivity. When that happens, you **reconnect and re-sign** to continue. No funds are ever involved.

Talk to Myrad applies reasonable limits to prevent abuse, so everyone gets a fair, responsive experience.
