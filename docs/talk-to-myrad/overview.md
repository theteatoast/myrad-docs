# What is Talk to Myrad

**Talk to Myrad** is an AI chat interface over your MYRAD data. You connect the apps you already use (Netflix, Spotify, Strava, Zomato, Uber, GitHub, Zepto) inside the [MYRAD app](https://myradhq.xyz), and Talk to Myrad lets you ask questions about that data in plain language.

It is a **personal-life companion**, not a general-purpose chatbot. Ask it for recommendations, budgeting, habit insights, planning, health and fitness, cooking ideas, or a second opinion on a decision, grounded in your data plus the wider world.

---

## A companion, deliberately scoped

Talk to Myrad is built for life and data questions. It will politely decline trivia, math, homework, and coding help, and steer the conversation back to your life and your data.

This keeps every conversation grounded in something real: your activity, or the anonymized behavior of the crowd.

---

## Two ways to ask

| Mode | UI label | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crowd** | "The crowd" | Anonymized, platform-wide patterns: averages, distributions, most-popular picks. Never an individual. |
| **Personal** | "Your data" | Only *your own* contributed data: your history, your points. Never anyone else's. |

Learn more in [The Two Modes](./modes).

---

## How it fits with MYRAD

Talk to Myrad is a companion to the main MYRAD platform. You **connect and verify apps once** in the MYRAD app, where you also contribute and earn, and Talk to Myrad reads that verified data to power the conversation. You never hand over passwords or full account access; verification happens once, on your side.

---

## Where it runs

Talk to Myrad is a **web app**, and the chat runs inside it. There is no public API, and the chat is not meant to be called directly. Getting started takes two things: a wallet that holds MYRAD, and, for personal questions, a MYRAD sign-in. See [Getting Access](./getting-access).
