# The Two Modes

Talk to Myrad answers in one of two modes. You choose which one you are asking in.

| Mode | UI label | What it answers | Requires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Crowd** | "The crowd" | Anonymized, platform-wide patterns: averages, distributions, popular picks. Never an individual. | Wallet access |
| **Personal** | "Your data" | Only *your own* contributed data: your history, your points. Never anyone else's. | Wallet access + sign-in |

---

## The crowd

Crowd mode answers questions about **anonymized, platform-wide behavior**. Think averages, distributions, and what is popular across everyone who contributes, never a single person's record.

**Example questions:**

- "What's the most-watched genre on Netflix this month?"
- "How often do people order food on weekends versus weekdays?"
- "What's a typical weekly running distance for active users?"
- "Which cuisines are trending in food orders right now?"

Crowd mode needs **holder access** (a MYRAD-holding wallet) and nothing else. It never requires a sign-in and never exposes an individual.

---

## Your data

Personal mode answers questions about **your own contributed data**, and only yours. It is grounded in the apps you have connected and verified in the MYRAD app.

**Example questions:**

- "How many hours of Netflix did I watch last month?"
- "What are my top artists on Spotify this year?"
- "How consistent has my running been over the last few weeks?"
- "How much have I spent on food orders, and how many points do I have?"

Personal mode needs **holder access plus your MYRAD sign-in**, so it can confirm the data is yours. It never reads anyone else's data, and your identity is never exposed to anyone else.

---

## Both modes cost one credit per message

Every message, in either mode, uses **1 credit**. See [Credits & Billing](./credits) for how credits work, and [Privacy & Safety](./privacy) for the guarantees behind each mode.
