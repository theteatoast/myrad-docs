# Supported Integrations

Talk to Myrad can answer questions about the apps you have connected and verified. **Seven providers** are supported today, with more on the way.

| Provider | What you can ask about |
| --- | --- |
| **Netflix** | Watch history: hours, genres, favorite series and movies |
| **Spotify** | Music library: playlists, top and followed artists, listening profile |
| **Strava** | Fitness: distance, activity frequency, consistency |
| **Zomato** | Food orders: spend, favorite cuisines and restaurants |
| **Uber** | Rides: trips, spend, distance |
| **GitHub** | Developer profile: contributions, followers, activity |
| **Zepto** | Grocery orders: basket size, spend |

*More integrations are on the way. You will see "More Coming Soon" in the app.*

---

## How to connect

You connect these in the **main MYRAD app**, the "verify once" step. There, you prove your activity with a zero-knowledge proof, contribute, and earn. Once connected, that data becomes available to Talk to Myrad in **both** the crowd and your-data modes.

- Each connected provider unlocks more you can ask about.
- Your raw data never leaves your device. Talk to Myrad works from verified data, not your passwords or accounts.

See the main platform's [How It Works](../how-it-works) for the verification flow, and [Privacy & Safety](./privacy) for the guarantees.
