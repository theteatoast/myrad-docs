# Credits & Billing

Talk to Myrad runs on **credits**. The rule is simple:

:::tip 1 credit = 1 message
Every message you send, in **both** the crowd and your-data modes, uses **1 credit** from your balance.
:::

---

## Free starter credits

When you first verify a MYRAD-holding wallet, you receive **free starter credits**, granted once, so you can begin chatting right away. See [Getting Access](./getting-access).

---

## Topping up with credit packs

When you need more, top up with a **credit pack**, paid in **USDC on Base**:

| Price | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| $5 | 100 |
| $10 | 220 (200 + 20 bonus) |
| $20 | 480 (400 + 80 bonus) |

:::note Prices can change
Pack prices and allotments may change over time. The **live buy screen** in the app is always the source of truth.
:::

---

## How buying works

1. **Pick a pack** in the app.
2. **Your wallet confirms a USDC transfer** on Base.
3. **Credits appear** once the payment confirms on-chain.

The transfer is **verified on-chain**: Talk to Myrad checks the real USDC payment rather than trusting the client. See [Privacy & Safety](./privacy) for why that matters.

---

## No charge for a failed answer

If a message produces no response, the credit is **automatically refunded**. You are only ever charged for answers you actually receive.
