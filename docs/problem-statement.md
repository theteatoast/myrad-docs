# Problem Statement

## 1. Data Is Controlled and Undervalued
Data has become one of the most valuable commodities in the digital economy.

Global data monetization markets are projected to surpass **$700 billion by 2030** (Statista, 2024), yet the individuals and small organizations who create that data capture almost none of the value.

Large platforms aggregate and centralize data, using it to train AI models, improve targeting, and drive massive profits. Creators have no ownership, no pricing control, and no transparent way to earn from their contributions.

If it is your data, why should corporations own it, train models on it, and make billions while you get nothing?

---

## 2. No Market Liquidity for Data
Data remains one of the largest *illiquid asset classes* in the world.
While tokenized asset markets could exceed **$3.8 trillion by 2030** (Boston Consulting Group), there is still no open, liquid market for datasets.

Unlike tokens or NFTs, data cannot be easily priced, traded, or discovered.
It sits locked inside servers, APIs, and private databases inaccessible to those who could benefit most.

We are tokenizing money, real-world assets, and art, yet the most valuable asset of all human data remains unmonetized.

---

## 3. Broken and Unsustainable Incentives
Traditional data platforms rely on one-time sales, static licenses, or indirect monetization through advertising.
This model discourages ongoing participation, limits recurring revenue for creators, and concentrates value in the hands of intermediaries.

The result is a fragile, extractive system where users lose trust, creators stop contributing, and innovation slows down.

---

## The Opportunity

A liquid, tokenized data economy can create a new incentive model for the digital age.

* **Ownership:** Give creators true, verifiable rights to their data.
* **Liquidity:** Enable fair pricing and open access through on-chain markets.
* **Alignment:** Reward all participants based on real usage and value creation.

Blockchain provides the foundation for this reset transparent ownership, programmable markets, and verifiable payouts.

---

## The Vision

Imagine uploading a dataset and turning it into a **datacoin** a tokenized data asset that can be traded, bought for access, or burned to download.

Just like minting a meme coin or NFT, except now it is your data generating real economic value.
Every dataset becomes a liquid, programmable unit of information with its own price discovery and demand signals.

The next trillion-dollar market will not come from banks or brands.
It will come from **human data** and this time, it should belong to the people who create it.
