# Problem Statement

## 1. Data Is Undervalued and Centralized
Most valuable data is created by individuals, researchers, and small organizations — yet large platforms capture nearly all of its financial value.  
Creators lack ownership, pricing control, and transparent ways to profit from their work.

## 2. No Market Liquidity for Datasets
Datasets cannot be traded easily, which prevents fair valuation and limits creator participation.  
Unlike tokens or NFTs, data lacks an open liquidity layer.

## 3. Unsustainable Incentives
Traditional data platforms depend on one-time sales or static licenses.  
This model discourages long-term creator engagement and fails to generate recurring demand.

---

## The Opportunity

A liquid, tokenized model for data can:

- Reward creators based on ongoing usage.  
- Provide transparent pricing through on-chain liquidity.  
- Align ecosystem participants through shared growth.
