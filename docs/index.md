# MYRAD Documentation

**Verified human data. Proven with zero-knowledge proofs, shared only as aggregates.**

MYRAD is a privacy-preserving data marketplace. People connect the everyday apps they already use and prove their real activity with zero-knowledge proofs. The raw data never leaves their device, and MYRAD never sees it. In return, contributors earn points, rewards, and on-chain incentives, and the verified signals they produce become datasets that teams can trust.

These docs cover two products built on the same foundation:

| Product | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **The MYRAD platform** | Contributors connect apps, verify activity, and earn. Organizations consume the aggregated signals they produce. |
| **Talk to Myrad** | An AI companion that answers natural-language questions about your MYRAD data and crowd patterns. |
