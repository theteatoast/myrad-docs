# Introduction

**MYRAD** turns verified digital activity into privacy-safe behavior signals, and rewards the people who create them.

AI systems, product teams, and modern companies increasingly depend on data to make decisions. Yet most available data is untrustworthy, legally risky, or disconnected from real human behavior. At the same time, the individuals who generate that behavior every day capture almost none of its value.

MYRAD introduces a different model. Contributors connect the apps they already use and prove their real activity with zero-knowledge proofs. The raw data never leaves their device, and MYRAD never sees it. What the platform produces instead is a set of small, aggregated, privacy-safe signals that organizations can rely on, and contributors are paid for every verified contribution.

---

## The MYRAD Approach

MYRAD is built on three principles:

| Principle | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Human behavior is valuable** | Everyday digital activity contains insights worth capturing |
| **Privacy is non-negotiable** | Users never submit raw data, only cryptographic proofs |
| **Signals beat raw data** | Aggregated patterns are more useful, and safer, than individual records |

---

## From Activity to Signals

MYRAD does not store or sell raw user activity. Verified activity is transformed into signals that are:

- **Aggregated**: combined across many users
- **Cohort-based**: grouped by behavior, not identity
- **Time-bounded**: relevant to specific windows
- **Decision-ready**: actionable for product and AI teams

These signals describe what typically happens within a group, never what any single person does.

---

## Verification Without Exposure

MYRAD uses zero-knowledge proofs, via the Reclaim Protocol, to confirm that activity is real without revealing personal information. This lets:

- Enterprises trust the data, because every signal is proof-backed
- Users retain privacy, because raw data never leaves the device
- MYRAD avoid handling sensitive identifiers entirely

See [Zero-Knowledge Verification](./zero-knowledge) for how this works.

## Contribute, and Earn

Contributors sit at the center of MYRAD. Once you connect a supported app and verify it, every verified contribution earns points, and points unlock rewards.

- **Points, streaks, and referrals** reward consistent participation
- **Lucky Spin, Inbox rewards, and the Store** turn points into value
- **A leaderboard and public metrics page** keep progress transparent
- **The MYRAD token** adds an on-chain rewards layer on Base, including staking and shared prize pools

Data sharing is always explicit opt-in. Contributors can opt out and remove their data at any time.

Read more in [Points & Rewards](./points-and-rewards) and [Token & Staking](./token).

## What Sets Us Apart

- **No raw data retention**: MYRAD never sees, stores, or processes personal data
- **Cohort aggregation**: all outputs represent group behavior
- **Consent-first design**: participation is voluntary and reversible
- **Compliance by default**: a GDPR- and CCPA-compatible architecture

The result is a different kind of data economy: signal sharing instead of data hoarding, consent instead of surveillance.
