# Token & Staking

MYRAD adds an **on-chain rewards layer** on top of the platform's points loop. It is built around the **MYRAD token on Base**, and it lets contributors put their participation to work through staking, shared prize pools, and dataset evaluation.

:::note
This page describes what the token layer does at a product level. On-chain details and live parameters are always shown in-product, on the relevant screens.
:::

---

## The MYRAD Token

MYRAD is an on-chain token deployed on **Base**. It anchors the rewards layer of the platform and gives contributors a way to participate in the value they help create. Verified contributions can be anchored on-chain, making participation publicly verifiable while keeping every individual's raw data private.

---

## Staking in Epochs

Contributors can **stake MYRAD** into **time-boxed epochs**. Each epoch is a defined window during which staked participants are eligible for rewards from a **shared prize pool**.

| Concept | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Epoch** | A fixed time window you stake into |
| **Prize pool** | A shared reward pool for that epoch's participants |
| **Proportional rewards** | Rewards are distributed proportionally among participants |
| **Payout** | Rewards can be paid in **MYRAD** or **USDC** |

Because rewards are shared across an epoch's participants and distributed proportionally, staking aligns everyone toward the same outcome: a healthy, active, verified data pool.

---

## Dataset Evaluation

Epochs also connect contributors to the datasets they help build. **Eligible participants can review the datasets tied to an epoch**, earn **evaluation points** for their contribution to quality, and **claim their rewards**.

This creates a feedback loop: the people who contribute data also help validate it, which keeps MYRAD's signals high-quality and trustworthy.

---

## Publicly Verifiable, Privately Sourced

The on-chain layer makes MYRAD's core promise concrete: contributions are provable in public and private by design at the same time.

- Participation and rewards are anchored **on-chain** for transparency
- The underlying activity is verified with **zero-knowledge proofs**, so raw data is never exposed
- Payouts settle on **Base**, in **MYRAD** or **USDC**

Read [Zero-Knowledge Verification](./zero-knowledge) for how contributions are proven without being revealed.
