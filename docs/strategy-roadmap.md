# Strategy and Roadmap

The MYRAD ecosystem grows through the **Creator–Liquidity–Utility Flywheel**, a self-reinforcing cycle that drives adoption, value creation, and long-term sustainability.

We are currently in **pre-beta**, with a **functional product deployed on Base Sepolia testnet** and **150 creators on the waitlist**.  
The focus now is on refining design, strengthening smart contracts, and preparing for mainnet readiness.

---

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: Beta Foundation (Months 0–3)
- Finalize core smart contracts and perform testnet audits.  
- Improve front-end design and UX for seamless dataset uploads.  
- Expand the beta waitlist to 500+ verified creators.  
- Begin early trading and feedback cycles on Base Sepolia.  

### Phase 2: Creator Acquisition (Months 3–6)
- Target high-quality data creators in AI, research, and analytics.  
- Launch zero-cost onboarding and datacoin minting.  
- Establish first creator leaderboard and reputation layer.  
- Start community-driven dataset discovery and curation.  

### Phase 3: Liquidity Bootstrapping (Months 6–9)
- Deploy liquidity incentives for early traders and data buyers.  
- Introduce trading rewards and dynamic fee sharing.  
- Move from Base Sepolia to **Base Mainnet**.  
- Begin protocol-owned liquidity seeding.  

### Phase 4: Utility Expansion (Months 9–15)
- Optimize burn mechanics and access workflows (under 3 seconds).  
- Launch dataset rating, tagging, and verification systems.  
- Enable cross-dataset bundling and token composability.  
- Introduce MYRAD governance and staking logic (v1).  

### Phase 5: Enterprise Monetization (Months 15+)
- Deploy **B2B APIs** for AI and data analytics companies.  
- Integrate verifiable dataset feeds with external AI agents.  
- Form strategic partnerships with research labs and data DAOs.  
- Establish ecosystem grant and co-creation programs.

## Long-Term Goal

To become the **decentralized data liquidity layer for AI, research, and machine learning**, empowering creators while enabling transparent and efficient data exchange across the Web3 ecosystem.  

MYRAD is building toward a future where **every dataset is a liquid asset**, every creator is an owner, and every AI model can access data through open, verifiable markets.
