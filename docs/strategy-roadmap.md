# Strategy and Roadmap

The MYRAD ecosystem is built around the **Creator-Liquidity-Utility Flywheel** — a cycle that continuously drives adoption and value creation.

---

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: Creator Acquisition (Months 1–3)
- Target niche, high-quality creators.
- Offer zero-cost onboarding and token minting.
- Establish initial trading activity.

### Phase 2: Liquidity Bootstrapping (Months 1–6)
- Use protocol funds to seed early pools.
- Incentivize participation through trading and staking rewards.

### Phase 3: Utility Validation (Months 4–12)
- Optimize burn mechanics.
- Streamline dataset access (under 3 seconds).
- Launch dataset rating and review systems.

### Phase 4: B2B Monetization (Month 7+)
- Deploy enterprise subscription APIs.
- Pilot verified dataset feeds for AI and analytics companies.

## Long-Term Goal

To become the **decentralized data liquidity layer for AI and research**, empowering creators while enabling transparent, efficient data exchange across the Web3 ecosystem.
