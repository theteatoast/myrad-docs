# For Enterprises

MYRAD offers **verified, consent-based datasets** as a compliant alternative to scraping: clean, high-signal data for teams that need to understand real human behavior without inheriting the legal and ethical risk of raw personal data.

Every dataset is proof-backed and aggregated. You get the answers, never the individual records.

---

## The Alternative to Scraping

Scraped data is untrustworthy, legally fragile, and increasingly contested. MYRAD replaces it with signals that are:

| Property | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified** | Every signal traces to a zero-knowledge proof of real activity |
| **Consent-based** | All data is explicitly opt-in from contributors |
| **Aggregated** | Cohort-level only; no individual is ever exposed |
| **Compliant** | Built for GDPR, CCPA, and future regulation |

You don't need raw data. You need reliable answers that hold up to scrutiny.
