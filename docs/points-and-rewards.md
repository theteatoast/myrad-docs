# Points & Rewards

Every verified contribution earns points. Points power the full rewards loop: streaks, referrals, Lucky Spin, Inbox reward cards, and the Store.

---

## Points

**Points are the core unit of contribution.** You earn them for each successful, on-device verification of a supported app.

- Points accrue in real time as your contributions are verified
- They reflect verified participation, not raw data; your activity stays private
- Your total is always visible on your **profile**

Because everything is proof-backed, points represent real human contribution, not scraped or synthetic activity.

---

## Streaks

Keeping a **streak** of ongoing contributions rewards contributors who show up regularly rather than just once. Streaks reward consistency over volume.

---

## Refer Friends

**Referrals** reward you for bringing in new contributors who verify their own activity, which expands the pool of privacy-safe signals for everyone.

---

## Lucky Spin

The **Lucky Spin** gives contributors a chance at bonus rewards on top of everyday earning.

---

## Inbox Rewards

Your **Inbox** delivers reward cards over time. Open them to claim what's inside.

---

## The Store

In the **Store**, you redeem your points for rewards.

---

## Leaderboard & Profile

MYRAD keeps progress transparent:

| Surface | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Leaderboard** | Where you stand among all contributors |
| **Profile** | Your points, streak, and contribution history |
| **Metrics page** | Public, platform-wide statistics anyone can view |

---

## Beyond Points: The Token Layer

Points are the on-platform reward loop. On top of them, MYRAD adds an **on-chain rewards layer** built around the MYRAD token on Base: staking, shared prize pools, and rewards that can be paid in MYRAD or USDC.

Continue to [Token & Staking](./token) to learn how the on-chain layer works.

---

:::info Your data, your choice
Contributing is always explicit opt-in. You can opt out at any time and remove your data. Earning on MYRAD never requires giving up control of your information.
:::
